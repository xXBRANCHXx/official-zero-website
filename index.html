Why ZERO

Built around better taste, easier routines, and products people can actually use every day.

ZERO is not just one syrup bottle. The brand spans kitchen-friendly syrups, carry-anywhere drops, and ZFIT functional products that help customers fit healthier choices into coffee, water, and daily routines.

Better-tasting sweetness

ZERO Syrup is positioned as a smoother alternative to the bitter aftertaste many customers associate with stevia-based products.

Portable convenience

ZERO Drops sweeten a drink with just 5 to 7 drops, giving customers a format that can travel anywhere.

Functional support

ZFIT expands beyond sweetness with prebiotic fiber syrup and ACVS for customers focused on overall wellness.

Local mission

The company mission is simple: make Indonesia healthier by making healthy choices easier.
