Experience

Designed like a product interface, not a brochure.

The composition now follows a Linear-style rhythm: short navigation, one centered hero, framed content surfaces, selective product imagery, then a sequence of modular sections that explain the product without drowning the page.

Framing

Every block lives inside a quiet bordered surface so the eye always knows where the next message starts.

Typography

Large headline, restrained body copy, and compact labels for the same editorial feel Linear uses.

Spacing

Long vertical breathing room keeps each section premium and reduces clutter on mobile.

Contrast

White surfaces replace the original dark treatment while preserving a sharp product-software aesthetic.

Structure

A layout system built for multiple ZERO product lines.

Narrative

From first impression to repeat purchase, the page now has a cleaner sequence.

The homepage moves from promise, to product system, to credibility, then to action. The goal is the same discipline you see on strong software landing pages, translated into a cleaner consumer brand context.

01. Hero

Open with one sharp promise and a clean product interface frame that immediately establishes confidence.

02. Explanation

Use short, bordered modules to explain how the brand works and why the layout feels intentional.

03. Conversion

End in a simple set of choices: explore the catalog, open ZFit, or message the team directly.
